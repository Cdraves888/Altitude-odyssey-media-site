Lil’ Bean Café is a Latin family-run business in the heart of Jacksonville, NC. We believe great coffee and good food should bring people together — whether you’re grabbing a quick breakfast burrito before work or settling in for a slow Saturday morning matcha.

From open mic nights to local art shows, our doors are always open. Come as you are.
