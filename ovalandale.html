Our Story

Where Ancient
Craft Meets
Coastal Living

Oval & Ale brings the tradition of Roman Pinsa to the heart of Hampstead, NC. Our dough — a centuries-old Italian recipe — produces a light, crispy oval crust unlike anything you'll find at an ordinary pizza place. Topped with the freshest ingredients and paired with a rotating selection of 12 craft beers, local wines, and seasonal cocktails, every visit is its own experience.

We believe great food is just the beginning. With live music most weekends, a welcoming dog-friendly patio, and a crew that genuinely loves what they do, Oval & Ale has become a gathering place for the whole community — families, friends, neighbors, and anyone passing through the coast looking for something real.
