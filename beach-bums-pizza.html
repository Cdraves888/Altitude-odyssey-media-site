Our Story

More Than Just a Pizza Joint.

Nestled right across from the Atlantic on Island Drive, Beach Bums Pizzeria, Bar & Grill is a locally owned, laid-back spot built for the way the beach makes you feel — relaxed, hungry, and ready for a good time.

Our kitchen fires up hand-tossed 16" pizzas loaded with bold toppings — from the iconic Topsail Special to the indulgent Hog Heaven. Beyond pizza, we've got burgers, steak nachos, wings, fresh salads, and a full breakfast menu every Thursday through Sunday.

When the sun goes down, the party picks up. The bar pours carefully crafted cocktails and cold beers while live music, Music Bingo, Karaoke, and Trivia Night keep the patio alive. Come as you are. Stay as long as you want.
