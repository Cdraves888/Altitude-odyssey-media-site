About Us

Born from the Ocean.
Built for the Night.

Opened in 2024, The Undertow is Surf City's first true nightclub experience — an oceanfront venue where the energy of the coast collides with the pulse of the night. Housed in a striking 4,000 sq ft space steps from the Atlantic, we've brought a world-class sound system, immersive lighting production, and a full VIP bottle service program to the Carolina coast.

We don't just open our doors — we create nights worth remembering.
