Our Story

More Than Just a Dog Stand.

Tucked right across the street from the beach on S Shore Drive, Hot Diggity Dogz has been serving up Surf City's most craveable hot dogs since day one. We're a colorful little stand with a big personality — and an even bigger menu.

Every dog starts with a fresh, all-beef frank and gets built to perfection with your choice of 13 different toppings. Whether you're going classic with yellow mustard and relish, loading up a Carolina Dog with chili sauce and slaw, or piling on everything you've got — we make it right.

But dogs are just the beginning. We've got BBQ, milkshakes, soft serve ice cream, mac-n-cheese, shaved ice, and even a veggie dog option. Owner Carroll and the crew are here all summer to make sure you leave happy. Come hungry.
