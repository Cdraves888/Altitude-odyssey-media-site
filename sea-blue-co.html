This Is Us

This Is Us.

"No Secret Ingredients."

We are a Family Owned & Operated Coffee and Ice Cream Shop located on Topsail Island. Our Shop, which offers a laid-back, beach vibe, has become one of the most popular spots for Coffee and Smoothies on the Island. We Proudly Serve Super Premium Award-Winning Ice Cream Crafted in Madison, Wisconsin.

Located in the heart of Surf City, we're just a short stroll from beach access 18 and 19. Come see us — locally roasted coffee, premium ice cream, unique gifts, and a whole lot of island love. 🏖️
