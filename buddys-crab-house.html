Right on the water. Right in Surf City.

Buddy's Crab House & Oyster Bar is a Surf City staple — right next to the Surf City Fishing Pier with one of the best views on Topsail Island. Catch the ocean breeze from our waterfront deck while you dig into the freshest seafood the coast has to offer.

The walls are covered in signed dollar bills — a Buddy's tradition going back years, all mounted on cedar wood sourced from the Cape Fear River. Each bill tells a story. Add yours.
