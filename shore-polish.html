Our Story

A Studio Built
on Care & Craft

Shore Polish was born from a simple belief: everyone deserves to feel genuinely taken care of. Founded by a Surf City local who grew up loving the laid-back coast as much as the art of a perfect set, this studio was designed to feel like coming home.

We're a small, intimate studio — just two nail techs who care deeply about their work and their clients. That means fresh tools every single appointment, zero rushing, and a genuinely relaxed experience from the moment you walk in.

We're appointment-only, so your time here is truly your own. No crowded waiting rooms, no noise — just you, your nails, and the quiet calm of the coast.

— Shore Polish, Surf City, NC
