Our Story

Rooted in Hampstead

"Rooted in family. Growing for you."

Pender Pines Garden Center was founded in 1996 by Gary and Sharon Schwarz, starting with one building and a two-hoop greenhouse. The initial team included their three children — Jon, Jessica, and Jason — while Gary's parents, Lily and Al, greeted customers as unofficial hosts.

In 2002, Jon Schwarz graduated from NC State's Horticulture program and founded the Landscape Department at Pender Pines, creating a ½ acre Arboretum. As the area grew, he expanded his vision to include a 7,200 sq. ft. retail greenhouse, 17,500 sq. ft. of heated growing houses, and over 8 acres for retail.

Nearly three decades later, Pender Pines remains a family-run destination for homeowners, landscapers, and plant lovers across Brunswick and Pender counties — open every single day, 9am to 5pm.
