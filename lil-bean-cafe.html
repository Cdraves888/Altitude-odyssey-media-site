Born in Downtown Jacksonville.
Built for the Whole Community.

Lil Bean Cafe is a local, family-owned cafe located in the heart of downtown Jacksonville, on Western Blvd. We strive to provide a warm and inviting atmosphere for both traveling customers and our local community.

We serve delicious breakfast sandwiches, burritos, and lunch offerings, all made with locally sourced ingredients to ensure quality and consistency. Our mission is to serve the best coffee in town, made with care and attention to detail.

At Lil Bean Cafe, we want our guests to feel at home. Our cafe is designed to be a warm and welcoming space where you can relax and enjoy a meal or a cup of coffee. We have both indoor and outdoor seating, and we offer free Wi-Fi for our guests.

What Makes Us Different

Our Concept

01

Coffee & Restaurant in One

Lil Bean Cafe is a unique cafe that combines the best of a coffee shop and a restaurant. We offer a full menu of delicious breakfast, lunch, and dinner items, as well as a wide selection of coffee and tea drinks. Whether you're looking for a quick bite or a relaxing meal, we have something for everyone.

02

Freshest Ingredients

We are committed to using only the freshest and highest quality ingredients in our dishes. We source our produce, meats, and dairy from local farms and suppliers, and we use organic and non-GMO ingredients whenever possible. Our chefs take pride in creating dishes that are both healthy and delicious.

03

A Welcoming Atmosphere

At Lil Bean Cafe, we want our guests to feel at home. Our cafe is designed to be a warm and welcoming space where you can relax and enjoy a meal or a cup of coffee. We have both indoor and outdoor seating, and we offer free Wi-Fi for our guests.
