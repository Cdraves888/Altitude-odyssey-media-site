About Us

The Heart of
Holly Ridge

Dale's Pub has been a cornerstone of Holly Ridge and Onslow County since the turn of the millennium. More than just a bar — we're a neighborhood institution. A place where locals gather after a long day, friends catch up over cold beers, and strangers become regulars.

Tucked along East Ocean Highway, we've built a reputation for cold drinks, honest service, and a no-frills, come-as-you-are atmosphere that keeps people coming back. Our nightclub setup means the party doesn't stop when the sun goes down — with a patio, pool tables, and a dance floor ready to go.

Over two decades in, we're still here. Still pouring cold ones. Still playing great music. Still the same spot Holly Ridge has always counted on for a good time.
